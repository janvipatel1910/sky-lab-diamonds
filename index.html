THE FACTS

Lab-Grown vs Mined

Both are real diamonds. The difference is where they come from.

Lab-grown diamond Mined diamond
What it is Real diamond: crystallised carbon Real diamond: crystallised carbon
Hardness and sparkle Same hardness (10 on the Mohs scale), same brilliance and fire Same hardness (10 on the Mohs scale), same brilliance and fire
How it forms Grown in weeks in a laboratory, using the HPHT or CVD process Formed deep underground over millions of years, then mined
Grading Graded on the 4Cs by independent labs such as IGI and GIA Graded on the 4Cs by independent labs such as IGI and GIA
Telling them apart Not by eye. Gem labs use specialist equipment; certificates state "laboratory-grown" Not by eye. Gem labs use specialist equipment to confirm natural origin
Price Typically much lower than a mined diamond of similar size and grade Higher, reflecting rarity and the cost of mining
Supply for retailers Consistent sizes and matching stones can be sourced to order Depends on what is mined and available
